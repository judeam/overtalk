Third-party connection logos

These assets identify services that users may choose to connect to Overtalk.
Their trademarks and artwork belong to their respective owners. They are not
covered by Overtalk's MIT license and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
The supplied artwork is displayed without recoloring or altering its geometry.

Linear: official Linear icon, from the publisher's brand asset archive.
https://linear.app/brand
https://static.linear.app/design-assets/Linear-Brand-Assets.zip?v=3

https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos/

Retrieved 16 October 2026. Assets are embedded locally; opening a connection
